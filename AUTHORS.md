# Authors

## Avanish Singh

**Project Author and Maintainer**  
Crown & Ledger Evidence Workspace

### Role & Contributions
Avanish Singh is the creator, principal architect, and maintainer of the **Crown & Ledger Evidence Workspace** — a secure digital document management and evidentiary integrity system built for legal practitioners, forensic investigators, law enforcement, and judicial benches.

Key responsibilities and contributions include:
- System conceptualization, technical architecture, and end-to-end design.
- Full-stack engineering spanning the React/TypeScript frontend, Express REST API backend, and PostgreSQL database schema.
- Implementation of the 8-persona Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model and database Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
- Design of cryptographic integrity controls, including SHA-256 binary hashing, immutable audit streams, and tamper-evident chain of custody ledgers.
- Implementation of specialized domain workflows: Section 65B Indian Evidence Act forensic certification, 8-stage judicial docket management, police FIR investigation intake, and strict citizen/victim data isolation.
- Quality assurance, live integration testing, and cryptographic verification suites.
